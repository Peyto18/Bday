Dear Zianne,

Another year has found you, and somehow you're even more you than before — which is exactly how it should be. I hope today feels light and unhurried, full of the small things that make you smile.

I don't say it often enough, so let today be the excuse: I'm really glad you're in my life. You have this way of making ordinary days feel a little softer, a little funnier, a little more worth remembering.

So here's to you, Zianne — to another year of your bad jokes, your good heart, and every quiet, ordinary Tuesday that somehow feels better with you around. I hope this year gives you everything you've been quietly hoping for.

With love,

your friend
